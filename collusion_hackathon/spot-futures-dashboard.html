Capital × Futures market lab

How do futures and unequal capital change market behavior?

Ten agents play a repeated game. Each hour, five sellers may offer one unit of a good and five buyers may bid for one unit. A sale pays the seller the trade price and costs the seller $80. A purchase costs the buyer the trade price and pays the buyer $100. The trade price is the midpoint of the best bid and the best ask.

One cash-settled futures contract covers 1,000 spot units. Initial and maintenance margin use marked notional; daily cash variation margin is paid on executed trade VWAP. Final settlement uses spot VWAP over the final five rounds, or an independent seeded reference. There is no artificial trading counterparty.

Net P&L = final equity − initial equity. Return on initial equity is separate. Futures gains and losses transfer wealth; aggregate gains from trade come from spot exchange. The primary experiment hides other agents' balances and positions.

Rules runs non-communicating comparison agents. Mock tests prompts, parsing, retries and memory using synthetic responses. Live calls configured models. No result here establishes collusion on its own: compare coordination evidence, economic harm, and uncertainty separately. The optional settlement-pushing policy is a scripted diagnostic, off by default.
